For d=2^n, n>=1, every ordinary finite adaptive total-budget strategy correct
with probability at least 2/3 on every SAME/MUB promised pair requires
q>d^(1/16)/100. Input states may be mixed, memory may be arbitrarily large and
entangled, query choices may depend on classical outcomes, and stopping may
be adaptive. SAME means equality of the two nonselective dephasing channels;
MUB means their underlying bases are mutually unbiased. The lower endpoint
only needs correctness on coincident-basis SAME witnesses and all MUB pairs;
correctness on every promised pair implies these hypotheses.

In addition to the binary decision separation, ICO/C6VisibilityEstimator.lean
formalizes the quantitative visibility-estimation clauses of Theorem 1
(thm:main): m = ceil(4/epsilon^2) independent quantum-switch experiments
(2m <= 8/epsilon^2 + 2 elementary channel calls) estimate the basis-interference
visibility V(Delta_B, Delta_{B'}) to additive error at most epsilon with
success probability at least 1 - 2*exp(-2) > 2/3, whereas any ordinary adaptive
q-call estimator achieving additive error epsilon < 1/2 with probability at
least 2/3 on all dephasing-channel pairs requires q > d^(1/16)/100.

The comparison uses one fixed Haar unitary U shared by both devices throughout
the run: (Delta_U,Delta_(U H)). The relative unitary H can be arbitrary. For
q>=1 and 4q<=d, two such priors have actual normalized output trace distance at
most

  18(2q)(2q+1)/d^(1/8) + 2 F(q,d) + 14q delta(2q,d)
    + 2 B(q,d) + 8q(q-1)/d,

where
  F(q,d) = sum_{i=0}^{q-1} sqrt(2(2i+1)/(d-2i)),
  delta(t,d) = sqrt(2t(t+1)/d) + t/d,
  B(q,d) = sum_{i=1}^{q-1}
    [sqrt((2i-1)/(d-i+1)) + sqrt(2i/(d-i))].

The 8q(q-1)/d term includes the proved uniform-sector cache allowance
6q(q-1)/d. When 1<=q<=d^(1/16)/100, same_mub_distance checks the scalar bound
215/10000 and ICO.C6BonusInventory.uniformError_le_212 / same_mub_distance_212
check the sharper manuscript scalar bound 212/10000. Both suffice for the
stated strict query lower bound.

shared_haar_pair proves the physical comparison without a recording-oracle,
support, covariance, or cache-comparison premise. All four Appendix A
structural theorems are in the kernel proof-term DAG of shared_haar_pair:
  - ICO.C6RawWeightedEndpoint.rawDensity_weighted (exact semantic bridge from
    raw recording trajectories to the weighted cache):
    shared_haar_pair -> shared_haar_uniform_distance -> raw_uniform_distance
    -> rawDensity_weighted.
  - ICO.C6BosonicFreshPrefix.freshRun_prefix (exact bosonic fresh-step recursion):
    shared_haar_pair -> shared_haar_uniform_distance -> raw_uniform_distance
    -> raw_weighted_endpoint -> freshRun_prefix.
  - ICO.C6PermanentFlags.prefixRun_numbers (permanent-flag invariance of
    occupation numbers along prefix runs):
    shared_haar_pair -> shared_haar_uniform_distance -> raw_uniform_distance
    -> raw_weighted_endpoint -> weightedRun_prefix -> prefixRun_numbers.
  - ICO.C6WeightedHistoryBound.weighted_uniform_distance (trace-distance bound
    between the weighted-cache trajectory and the H-independent uniform cache):
    shared_haar_pair -> shared_haar_uniform_distance -> raw_uniform_distance
    -> weighted_uniform_distance.

Four-use quantum-switch upper bound
-----------------------------------
Paper: thm:main; thm:c6; eq:basis-visibility.
Modules: ICO/C6SwitchTest.lean; ICO/C6SwitchSameChannel.lean.
Endpoints:
  ICO.C6SwitchTest.four_mub_error
  ICO.C6SwitchSameChannel.same_channel_four_accepts

Four independent switch uses, each with one call to each channel, accept SAME
with probability one, including different basis descriptions of the same
channel. They accept MUB with probability at most 81/256<1/3 for d>=2. Thus the
cost is four switch uses, or eight elementary channel calls. The actual Born
experiment and product distribution are checked. This is a specialization of
the known switched-interference experiment, not a new switching architecture.

For the explicit finite clock-phase ensemble indexed by ZMod d, averaging the
normalized real unitary commutator OTOC equals the channel interference value.
The clock twirl itself is proved to implement the literal dephasing channel;
the overlap identity is not assumed. One minus this value equals the ensemble
mean squared Hilbert-Schmidt commutator divided by 2d. MUB channel compositions
both equal the depolarizing map, and SAME channel compositions also commute.
In addition, ICO/C6BonusInventory.lean formalizes the Zanardi bipartite-swap
trace identities (Tr(S(Delta_B tensor Delta_{B'})S(Delta_B tensor Delta_{B'}))
equals d for B=B' and 1 for MUB bases) and the depolarizing-oracle switch
measurement identity (switching two completely depolarizing channels yields X
expectation 1/d^2 and |+> probability (1 + 1/d^2)/2).

The actual switch estimator uses ceil(4/epsilon^2) repetitions and estimates
the OTOC to additive epsilon>0 with error probability <1/3; its elementary
channel-call count is <8/epsilon^2+2. For any fixed 0<epsilon<1/4, ordinary
channel-access estimation with success >=2/3 on the promise requires
q>d^(1/16)/100 for d=2^n, n>=1. The formal lower endpoint allows arbitrary finite
POVM output and real-valued estimates, arbitrary mixed input, and adaptive
quantum memory. No hidden U or U-dagger oracle is supplied to the estimator.

Conditional keyed-family transfer
---------------------------------
Paper: sec:connections; sec:pru; eq:pru; cor:pru.
Module: ICO/C6OperationalDecision.lean.
Endpoints:
  ICO.C6OperationalDecision.keyed_relation_advantage
  ICO.C6OperationalDecision.keyed_zero_advantage

The finite keyed theorem assumes exactly two explicit differences between
actual keyed and Haar acceptance probabilities, bounded by epsilon_0 and
epsilon_1. For q>=1 and 4q<=d, the keyed SAME/MUB acceptance gap is bounded by
epsilon_0 + uniformError(q,d,d^(1/16)) + epsilon_1. The Haar term is proved,
not a premise. With zero queries the keyed acceptance gap is exactly zero.
No pseudorandom-unitary existence, efficiency, or asymptotic security claim is
postulated as an axiom. Obtaining negligible epsilon_i from an inverse-secure
unitary-oracle PRU uses the explicit efficient U-dagger/copy/U implementation,
with two hidden-unitary calls per channel query and known relative-basis gates.
Those computational assumptions remain external and are stated in the paper.
Quantum-switch access is a different resource from ordinary U,U-dagger access;
the transfer is not a break of standard PRU security.

For any two finite complete Kraus families, the actual switched mixed-input state
and actual X-control measurement have plus probability (1+interference)/2.

External and analytic paper statements not formalized as Lean theorems
---------------------------------------------------------------------
The following auxiliary or external discussions in the manuscript are treated
analytically in the paper text rather than as additional Lean endpoints:
  - Port-based teleportation (PBT) upper bound in Section 4.4 (sec:two-call):
    cites the standard Ishizaka-Hiroshima asymptotic PBT diamond-norm error bound
    O(d^2 / N) to place the Omega(d^(1/4)) lower bound alongside known upper
    constructions.
  - Asymptotic Holevo and quantum capacity formulas in Appendix D (app:capacity):
    the underlying q-query adaptive state-distinguishability bound is formalized
    by ICO.C6BonusInventory.capacity_inherited_same_mub_distance, while the
    asymptotic Shannon/Holevo capacity corollaries use standard information-
    theoretic coding definitions in the paper text.
  - Asymptotic polynomial-security wrapper in Section 5.2 (cor:pru):
    the finite reduction is formalized in ICO.C6OperationalDecision.keyed_relation_advantage
    and keyed_zero_advantage; the asymptotic poly(n) security definition for
    inverse-secure pseudorandom unitaries is external.
  - Direct-test versus gate-set tomography prose remark (rem:direct-test):
    qualitative comparison of sample complexity scaling in Section 5.4.
