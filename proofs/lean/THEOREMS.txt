Definition 4 (def:ordinary, sec:model) is represented throughout the formal
development by `ICO.BudgetQueryTree.Tree` (finite total call budget across both
boxes, arbitrary mixed initial state prepared via `Tree.prepare` and
`Tree.prepare_channel`, arbitrary finite quantum memory register, classical
outcome-adaptive box selection, and early stopping), together with the labelled
per-box budget trees (`ICO.AtMostLabelledQueryTree` and `ICO.LabelledQueryTree`)
used in the structural decompositions. Simulation error (eq:simulation-error)
is formalized as `QIT.Channel.diamondTraceDistance` (one half of the diamond
norm). Continuity of Choi operators along budget trees is proved in
`Tree.channel_choi_continuous` and `uniformChannel_choi_continuous`.

Section 2.5 ("What the switch measures: the visibility", sec:visibility,
eq:visibility, eq:conjugation, eq:basis-visibility) is formalized in
`ICO/VisibilityIdentities.lean`:
  - `basis_dephasing_visibility` proves the basis-dephasing visibility formula
    (eq:basis-visibility) for every pair of unitaries U, U' in arbitrary finite
    dimension d >= 1:
    v(Delta_U, Delta_{U'}) = (1/d) sum_{j,k} |<j | U^dagger U' | k>|^4.
  - `one_sub_interference_eq_commutator_hs`,
    `one_sub_interference_eq_commutator_entry_sq`, and
    `one_sub_interference_eq_commutator_hilbertSchmidtSq` prove for arbitrary
    finite complete Kraus families {A_i} and {B_j} that
    1 - v(A, B) = (1/(2d)) sum_{i,j} ||[A_i, B_j]||_HS^2.
  - `haar_average_dephasing_eq_depolarizing` and
    `haar_average_dephasing_eq_depolarizingChannel` prove that averaging
    nonselective basis dephasing Delta_U over the Haar measure on U(d) yields
    the matched-fidelity depolarizing channel Lambda = (id + d Omega)/(d + 1)
    (also invoked in Section 5.1, sec:switch-test).

Lemma 5 (lem:ordered, sec:ordered, eq:vector-link, eq:ordered-comb) is
formalized in `ICO/PolarizationOrderedArbitraryControl.lean` both for single-use
normal-form trees (`ordered_components_decomposition`,
`ordered_components_decomposition_dim_d`, `tree_ordered_components`,
`tree_ordered_components_dim_d`) and directly for strategies that call each box
at most once on every run (`padAtMostOnce_channel_map`,
`atMostOnce_ordered_components`, `atMostOnce_ordered_components_dim_d`),
decomposing the strategy Choi operator into positive semidefinite ordered
components C_{AB} + C_{BA} satisfying the causal partial-trace constraints
(eq:ordered-comb) with weights p_1 + p_2 = 1 and reproducing the output channel
by Choi/Kraus link contraction.

Theorem 1 (thm:exact-simulation, sec:exact — "Exact simulation: no finite
number of calls suffices"): for every finite target dimension d >= 2 and every
finite total call budget q, no ordinary adaptive strategy with arbitrary finite
quantum memory realizes the quantum switch across all pairs of d-dimensional
channels (so the worst-case query complexity of exact simulation is infinite).
`ICO.BoundedSwitchObstruction.no_bounded_circuit` proves impossibility even when
the control qubit is initialized in |+> and retained at the output
(eq:switch-plus), using two-dimensional sector embedding (sec:embedding);
`no_bounded_full_switch_circuit` and `no_bounded_circuit_arbitrary_control`
prove impossibility for the full control-and-target quantum switch (eq:switch),
and `no_labelled_full_switch_circuit` proves impossibility under arbitrary
finite per-box call budgets (M, N).

Lemma 6 (lem:polarization, sec:polarization, eq:dilution):
`sum_perm_one_slot`, `sum_perm_two_slot`, `average_perm_two_slot`,
`symmetrized_polarization`, and `symmetrized_switch_polarization*` formalize
multilinear slot polarization, and `dilutedPlusSwitchMap_eq_four_terms`,
`one_call_each_diluted_switch_plus`, and `one_call_each_diluted_switch_full`
prove Lemma 6 directly in the manuscript's diluted-switch form: for a strategy
with M labelled A-slots and N labelled B-slots matching the switch on all
channel pairs, averaging uniformly over one active A-slot and one active B-slot
with identity channels in all remaining slots yields a one-call-each strategy
for the diluted switch T_{1/M, 1/N}[A, B] (both for fixed |+> control and for
arbitrary control inputs).

Remark 10 (rem:unitary — "One unitary box is easy", sec:where-channels,
fig:unitary):
  - `diluted_unitary_not_unitary` shows that for 0 < x < 1 the diluted Pauli-X
    box x Ad(sigma_x) + (1 - x) id is not a unitary channel.
  - When box A is known to be unitary and B is any CPTP channel, the explicit
    three-call controlled-swap dummy circuit `unitaryThreeCallTree` (fig:unitary,
    two calls to A and one call to B) simulates the quantum switch with zero
    error (`unitaryThreeCallTree_unitary_kraus_eq`,
    `unitaryThreeCallTree_unitary_left_channel_eq`, and the wire-reindexed
    `Fin 2 x Fin d` equality `unitaryThreeCallTree_fullSwitchChannelMap_eq`;
    for two unitary boxes, `unitaryThreeCallTree_channel_eq`,
    `unitaryThreeCallTree_fullChannel_eq`, `unitaryThreeCallTree_allChannel_eq`,
    and `unitaryThreeCallTree_diamond_zero`).
  - Symmetrically, when box B is known to be unitary and A is any CPTP channel,
    the role-exchanged three-call circuit `unitarySecondThreeCallTree` (two calls
    to B and one call to A) simulates the quantum switch with zero error
    (`unitarySecondThreeCallTree_unitary_kraus_eq` and
    `unitarySecondThreeCallTree_unitary_right_channel_eq`).
  - When the twice-called box A is not unitary, `unitaryThreeCallTree` fails to
    reproduce the switch (`unitaryThreeCallTree_fails_nonunitary` for qubit
    dephasing across every initial dummy basis state, and
    `exists_nonunitary_unitaryThreeCallTree_ne_channelSwitch`).

Theorem 2 (thm:simulation-summary, eq:tradeoff-intro, sec:approximation):
`ICO.C4PhysicalEnsemble.exists_full_switch_error_bound` proves that for every
target dimension d >= 2, every budget-q ordinary strategy, and every ensemble
size r >= 1 (parameterized in `C4PhysicalEnsemble` as `r + 1`), there exist
mixed-unitary channels A, B (drawn as uniform mixtures of r unitaries) on which
the strategy's half-diamond simulation error for the full-control quantum switch
is at least

  1/2 - 13 r^2 / (3 d) - q (q - 1) / (2 r).

`full_switch_fixed_accuracy_constant` derives the Omega_epsilon(d^(1/4)) total
call lower bound for every fixed error threshold epsilon < 1/2, and
`full_switch_one_over_48` proves the explicit numerical consequence: if d >= 64
and uniform simulation error is at most 1/48, then d < 4096 q^4.

Supporting lemmas and claims of Section 4 (`ICO/C4NamedClaims.lean`, building on
`ICO/CompositionGramAverage.lean`, `ICO/GramWitness.lean`,
`ICO/ReverseFidelity.lean`, `ICO/AverageCoherence.lean`, and
`ICO/FreshBudgetCompile.lean`):
  - Lemma 11 (lem:gram, sec:witness, eq:effect, eq:block-form, eq:gram):
    `lem_gram_near_orthonormality` (parameterized literally by r : Nat) and
    `lem_gram_near_orthonormality_sample` prove E ||K^dagger K - I|| <= r^2 / d
    for all three composed Kraus matrices S_0, S_1, and S.
  - Claim 12 (clm:switch-passes, eq:R1): `clm_switch_passes` (for 1 <= r) and
    `clm_switch_passes_sample` prove E Tr(Theta rho_sw) >= 1 - r^2 / d on the
    Choi input of the quantum switch.
  - Lemma 13 (lem:reverse, sec:reverse, eq:bd, eq:frame, eq:envelope):
    `reverse_test_trace_mono`, `causal_reverse_test_subcomb_bound`, and
    `lem_reverse_ordered_subcomb` combine `ICO.ReverseFidelity.causal_reverse_test_bound`
    and `ICO.ReverseFidelity.outcome_reverse_test_bound` with subcomb
    monotonicity for 0 <= C' <= C, giving the exact bound
    (Tr C / d^3) * (1/d + d/(d^2 - 1))^2.
  - Corollary 14 (cor:coherence, eq:coherence): `cor_coherence_at_most_once`
    (with expected-norm strengthening `cor_coherence_at_most_once_norm` and
    tree variants `cor_coherence_optional_two_query`, `cor_coherence_two_query`)
    proves as one unified statement that for any strategy calling each of U and
    V at most once on control input |+> and half of a maximally entangled state,
    the modulus of the Haar average of <VU | sigma_{01} | UV> is at most
    1/d + d/(d^2 - 1) (via `ICO.AverageCoherence.average_controlCoherence_bound`).
  - Claim 15 (clm:fresh, sec:hidden, eq:R2): `clm_fresh_distance`,
    `clm_fresh_channel_diamond_distance`, and `clm_fresh_trace_bound` prove the
    fresh-index replacement bound q(q - 1)/(2 r) (with paper r = Lean r + 1)
    both in normalized probe trace distance, in half-diamond channel distance,
    and in witness expectation (eq:R2).
  - Claim 16 (clm:pair, eq:R3): `clm_pair_fresh_coherence`,
    `clm_pair_fresh_coherence_norm`, `clm_pair_fresh_coherence_sum`, and
    `eq_R3_fresh_frame_bound` establish the per-pair Haar coherence bound
    1/d + d/(d^2 - 1) in the fresh-index process, its r^2 sum over index pairs,
    and the resulting frame expectation bound (eq:R3).

Remark 17 (rem:approx-content):
`exists_two_query_switch_error_ge_235` and `two_query_tradeoff_nonpos_of_le_234`
verify that for q = 2 the trade-off eq:tradeoff-intro first becomes positive at
d = 235 (at paper r = 3, giving 1/6 - 39/d >= 1/1410 > 0), while
`full_switch_one_over_48_three_queries_of_ge_65536` gives the q = 3 threshold
d >= 65536 at error 1/48. In addition,
`uniform_unitary_mixture_eq_oracleChannel_iff`,
`uniform_haar_mixture_ae_not_ambient_unitary`, and
`uniform_haar_mixture_ae_not_unitary` prove that for d >= 2 and r >= 2, the
uniform mixture of r independent Haar unitaries is almost surely not a unitary
channel.

Section 4.4 (sec:simulation — Upper bounds):
`ICO/Section44TwoCall.lean` (`dephaseControl_diamond_id_le_half`,
`twoCallTree_krausChannel_eq`, `twoCallTree_krausChannel_error_le_half`,
`twoCallTree_fullCircuit_eq`, `twoCallTree_fullSwitch_error_le_half`) constructs
the explicit two-call strategy that measures the control qubit in the
computational basis and applies the two boxes in the corresponding definite
order, proving that it equals the Z-dephased switch and achieves universal
half-diamond error at most 1/2. The asymptotic O(d^2 / epsilon) upper bound via
port-based teleportation discussed alongside it in Section 4.4 (sec:simulation)
is cited from Pirandola et al. (2019) and is not formalized as a Lean theorem.
