SAME versus MUB: the main quantum-switch separation (C6)
-------------------------------------------------------
Paper: thm:c6; sec:model; sec:c6-proof; lem:c6-comparison;
eq:c6-comparison; sec:c6-recording.
Modules: ICO/C6OperationalDecision.lean; ICO/C6SharedPairBound.lean;
ICO/C6RawWeightedEndpoint.lean.
Selected endpoints:
  ICO.C6OperationalDecision.same_mub_query_lower_bound
  ICO.C6OperationalDecision.mixed_tree_comparison
  ICO.C6OperationalDecision.adaptive_pair_distance
  ICO.C6OperationalDecision.adaptive_pair_distance_explicit
  ICO.C6OperationalDecision.same_mub_distance
  ICO.C6SharedPairBound.shared_haar_pair
  ICO.C6RawWeightedEndpoint.rawDensity_weighted

For d=2^n, n>=1, every ordinary finite adaptive total-budget strategy correct
with probability at least 2/3 on every SAME/MUB promised pair requires
q>d^(1/16)/100. Input states may be mixed, memory may be arbitrarily large and
entangled, query choices may depend on classical outcomes, and stopping may
be adaptive. SAME means equality of the two nonselective dephasing channels;
MUB means their underlying bases are mutually unbiased. The lower endpoint
only needs correctness on coincident-basis SAME witnesses and all MUB pairs;
correctness on every promised pair implies these hypotheses.

The comparison uses one fixed Haar unitary U shared by both devices throughout
the run: (Delta_U,Delta_(U H)). The relative unitary H can be arbitrary. For
q>=1 and 4q<=d, two such priors have actual normalized output trace distance at
most

  18(2q)(2q+1)/d^(1/8) + 2 F(q,d) + 14q delta(2q,d)
    + 2 B(q,d) + 8q(q-1)/d,

where
  F(q,d) = sum_{i=0}^{q-1} sqrt(2(2i+1)/(d-2i)),
  delta(t,d) = sqrt(2t(t+1)/d) + t/d,
  B(q,d) = sum_{i=1}^{q-1}
    [sqrt((2i-1)/(d-i+1)) + sqrt(2i/(d-i))].

The 8q(q-1)/d term includes the proved uniform-sector cache allowance
6q(q-1)/d. The checked scalar endpoint uses 215/10000 when
1<=q<=d^(1/16)/100. The manuscript also gives a sharper elementary estimate
212/10000; the formal numerical endpoint intentionally uses the weaker
215/10000. Both suffice for the stated strict query lower bound.

shared_haar_pair proves the physical comparison without a recording-oracle,
support, covariance, or cache-comparison premise. rawDensity_weighted is the
exact semantic bridge from actual raw recording trajectories to the weighted
cache, retaining both measurement-outcome and retrieval/birth histories until
the weights can be erased. Its cutoff and dimension conditions are discharged
in the selected physical endpoint. The protocol-tail type-level length is
independent of the number n of executed calls; the compiled q+1-call circuit
uses its tail of type-level length q at time n=q+1.

Four-use quantum-switch upper bound
-----------------------------------
Paper: thm:c6; eq:basis-visibility.
Modules: ICO/C6SwitchTest.lean; ICO/C6SwitchSameChannel.lean.
Endpoints:
  ICO.C6SwitchTest.four_mub_error
  ICO.C6SwitchSameChannel.same_channel_four_accepts

Four independent switch uses, each with one call to each channel, accept SAME
with probability one, including different basis descriptions of the same
channel. They accept MUB with probability at most 81/256<1/3 for d>=2. Thus the
cost is four switch uses, or eight elementary channel calls. The actual Born
experiment and product distribution are checked. This is a specialization of
the known switched-interference experiment, not a new switching architecture.

Phase-averaged OTOC and channel commutation
------------------------------------------
Paper: sec:connections; eq:otoc; cor:otoc.
Modules: ICO/C6OTOC.lean; ICO/C6OTOCCommutator.lean;
ICO/C6OTOCChannelBoundary.lean; ICO/C6OTOCEstimator.lean;
ICO/C6OperationalDecision.lean.
Endpoints:
  ICO.C6OTOC.phaseOTOC_eq_interference
  ICO.C6OTOC.phaseOTOC_born
  ICO.C6OTOCCommutator.phaseOTOC_complement
  ICO.C6OTOCChannelBoundary.mub_channels_commute
  ICO.C6OTOCChannelBoundary.same_channels_commute
  ICO.C6OTOCEstimator.switch_estimation
  ICO.C6OTOCEstimator.call_count
  ICO.C6OperationalDecision.otoc_query_lower_bound

For the explicit finite clock-phase ensemble indexed by ZMod d, averaging the
normalized real unitary commutator OTOC equals the channel interference value.
The clock twirl itself is proved to implement the literal dephasing channel;
the overlap identity is not assumed. One minus this value equals the ensemble
mean squared Hilbert-Schmidt commutator divided by 2d. MUB channel compositions
both equal the depolarizing map, and SAME channel compositions also commute.
The statistic therefore concerns Kraus-product interference, not failure of
the two channel superoperators to commute.

The actual switch estimator uses ceil(4/epsilon^2) repetitions and estimates
the OTOC to additive epsilon>0 with error probability <1/3; its elementary
channel-call count is <8/epsilon^2+2. For any fixed 0<epsilon<1/4, ordinary
channel-access estimation with success >=2/3 on the promise requires
q>d^(1/16)/100 for d=2^n, n>=1. The formal lower endpoint allows arbitrary finite
POVM output and real-valued estimates, arbitrary mixed input, and adaptive
quantum memory. No hidden U or U-dagger oracle is supplied to the estimator.

Conditional keyed-family transfer
---------------------------------
Paper: sec:connections; eq:pru.
Module: ICO/C6OperationalDecision.lean.
Endpoints:
  ICO.C6OperationalDecision.keyed_relation_advantage
  ICO.C6OperationalDecision.keyed_zero_advantage

The finite keyed theorem assumes exactly two explicit differences between
actual keyed and Haar acceptance probabilities, bounded by epsilon_0 and
epsilon_1. For q>=1 and 4q<=d, the keyed SAME/MUB acceptance gap is bounded by
epsilon_0 + uniformError(q,d,d^(1/16)) + epsilon_1. The Haar term is proved,
not a premise. With zero queries the keyed acceptance gap is exactly zero.
No pseudorandom-unitary existence, efficiency, or asymptotic security claim is
postulated as an axiom. Obtaining negligible epsilon_i from an inverse-secure
unitary-oracle PRU uses the explicit efficient U-dagger/copy/U implementation,
with two hidden-unitary calls per channel query and known relative-basis gates.
Those computational assumptions remain external and are stated in the paper.
Quantum-switch access is a different resource from ordinary U,U-dagger access;
the transfer is not a break of standard PRU security.

Exact universal simulation (P3)
------------------------------
Paper: thm:exact-simulation; sec:exact; finite certificate in sec:certificate
and lem:exact-obstruction.
Module: ICO/BoundedSwitchObstruction.lean
Endpoint: ICO.BoundedSwitchObstruction.no_bounded_circuit

For d>=2, every finite adaptive BudgetQueryTree with one total finite call budget
fails to realize the universal switch on all pairs of d-dimensional channels.
The statement includes arbitrary finite nonempty private memory. RealizesSwitch
is equality of actual channel maps on all finite complete Kraus families.
The checked endpoint already forbids exact simulation with input control fixed
to |+> and retained at output, for every target input. Therefore an arbitrary-
control universal simulator is impossible by preparing |+> before it. The
input-control quantifier is not part of no_bounded_circuit itself.
Its proof connects the actual ordinary circuit to polarization and the exact
qubit causal-support certificate. It does not assume the semantic reduction.

Approximate universal simulation (C4)
------------------------------------
Paper: thm:simulation-summary; sec:approximation; eq:tradeoff-intro.
Module: ICO/C4FullSwitchTheorem.lean
Endpoints:
  ICO.C4PhysicalEnsemble.exists_full_switch_error_bound
  ICO.C4PhysicalEnsemble.full_switch_fixed_accuracy_constant
  ICO.C4PhysicalEnsemble.full_switch_one_over_48

The first theorem supplies actual uniform-unitary-mixture channels on which a
given q-total-query finite adaptive circuit's error for the standard full-control
switch is at least 1/2 - 13r^2/(3d) - q(q-1)/(2r), with paper r=Lean r+1.
The metric is half the diamond norm (Channel.diamondTraceDistance).
The second proves Omega_epsilon(d^(1/4)) total calls for every fixed epsilon<1/2.
The third gives the explicit paper consequence: d>=64 and uniform error<=1/48
imply d<4096 q^4. The proof includes actual normalized Choi witnesses, Haar
moments, adaptive selected-pair extraction, and the total-query collision bound.

Dimension-independent switched testing upper bound (prior-work protocol)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
Paper: thm:testing; sec:robust (the known interference test).
Module: ICO/SwitchExperiment.lean
Endpoints:
  ICO.SwitchExperiment.switch_eighty_high_error
  ICO.SwitchExperiment.switch_eighty_low_error

For any two finite complete Kraus families, the actual switched mixed-input state
and actual X-control measurement have plus probability (1+interference)/2.
Eighty independent experiments, accepting high when at least 60 plus outcomes
occur, have error <1/3 for interference>=2/3 or interference<=1/3 respectively.
This checks the physical experiment and the sampling rule, not just an abstract
Bernoulli estimate. The manuscript credits the estimator to prior work.

Haar recording comparison used by C5
------------------------------------
Modules: ICO/MHHaarRecordingComparison.lean; ICO/MHProtocolHaarRecording.lean
Endpoints:
  ICO.MHHaarRecordingComparison.frequency_visible_distance
  ICO.MHProtocolHaarRecording.protocol_haar_recording

The generic signed protocol comparison has actual normalized trace distance
<=9t(t+1)/R between the product-Haar-averaged physical frequency oracle output and
the literal recording oracle's visible output, where d=R^8, R>=1, 2t<=d, t<d.
Known isometries may be rectangular, and public/private-copy memory varies at
every step. All moment, domain, inverse-query, support, covariance, norm-loss,
continuity, and overlap estimates are discharged in the theorem.

The protocol specialization uses the literal hidden-outcome rank-one measurement
implementation U^dagger/copy/U, two unitary queries per measurement-channel call.
For n channel calls and 4n<=d it gives 9(2n)(2n+1)/d^(1/8), comparing the actual
Haar-basis protocol density with the literal recording trajectory. The hidden
measurement label is private throughout, and final erasure is proved explicitly.

C5 adaptive fixed-basis indistinguishability
-------------------------------------------
Archival C5 comparison retained in the proof package. The former labels
thm:basis, sec:hidden-basis and eq:distance refer to the earlier manuscript,
not sections or numbered statements in the current C6-led paper.
Module: ICO/OrdinaryHaarStates.lean
Endpoint: ICO.OrdinaryHaarStates.adaptive_haar_distance

For arbitrary mixed input State, actual finite adaptive total-query-budget tree,
q>=1 and 2q<d, the actual output averaged over one fixed hidden Haar basis is
within normalized trace distance

  18(2q)(2q+1)/d^(1/8) + 4q/sqrt(d-2q) + q^2/d

of the same circuit with each call replaced by the depolarizing channel. There
is no supplied transcript, overlap, or recording comparison premise. The exact
fractional-power denominators agree with the archival C5 statement; this is not only a version
conditional on an auxiliary eighth- or sixteenth-root parameter. The physical
Haar-recording estimate also has a separately proved full-range trivial branch.
Additional selected endpoints retained from that proof are
ICO.OrdinaryHaarIndistinguishability.protocol_haar_recording_full and
ICO.OrdinaryHaarIndistinguishability.circuit_haar_bound.

C5 ordinary-query testing lower bound
-------------------------------------
Paper: thm:testing; sec:robust (constant-gap interference testing).
Module: ICO/C5Decision.lean
Endpoints:
  ICO.C5Decision.main_lower_bound
  ICO.C5Decision.measurement_only_lower_bound

For an arbitrary actual BinaryHypothesisTest applied to the output of an actual
q-total-query adaptive tree on an arbitrary mixed State input, correctness on
the hard promised families implies q > d^(1/16)/100. The main witness family
uses coincident hidden-basis measurement channels versus depolarizing channels.
The measurement-only lower bound applies to any algorithm correct on every
promised pair of rank-one nonselective measurements, for d>=4. The formal
endpoint needs correctness on all coincident-basis high witnesses and every
measurement pair with interference<=1/3; correctness on all promised pairs
implies those premises. Independent Haar bases are a hard distribution used
inside the proof, not a promise or restriction on the input pair. Measurement
outcomes are inaccessible to the algorithm; arbitrary known operations, quantum
memory, adaptive classical branches, and early stopping remain allowed.

The endpoints require only the stated test correctness probabilities on actual
channels. They do not assume SingleHaarBound, a trace-distance estimate, the
Ma-Huang theorem, or an abstract causal-order inequality. Those physical and
analytic inputs are discharged by the included proof chain. The real power
d^(1/16) appears directly; no auxiliary-radius premise remains.
