Trust boundary
==============

`#print axioms` computes transitive declaration dependencies, rather than merely
searching the visible proof text. The accepted axiom set for every final endpoint
is {propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound}. This is stronger evidence than the
source scan alone. The source scan independently rejects sorry, admit, custom
axiom declarations, native_decide and unsafe tokens outside comments/strings.

Finite exact certificates are checked with ordinary kernel-reduced decide and
integer algebra. Haar moments, channel/comb semantics, adaptive query compilation,
recording-oracle comparisons and norm bounds are proved from the included source
and pinned mathematical foundations; they are not postulated as physical axioms.

This file records dependency identity, not a claim to have separately re-proved
Lean's implementation or the soundness of its standard logical axioms.
