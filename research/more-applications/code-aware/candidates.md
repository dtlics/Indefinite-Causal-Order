**Priority discipline:** the remaining target is a naturally opaque QC/QEC operation with a useful consequence and a large matched advantage. The positive coherent-error construction is a mechanism to reuse, not an achieved answer. The new ordinary simulators and decoding upper bounds are filters for future searches, not grounds for abandoning the search.
