# Objective-specific Jacobi/coset form of the universal completion

This is an exact normal form of the dual optimization, together
with a finite-support annihilator formula. It does not supply a matching
positive completion or an exact GYNI value.

## 1. Output-dephased completions lose no unrestricted optimum

The flag-controlled local output dephasing in
`output_eigenvalue_reduction.md` also acts on an NS CP-channel completion F.
It preserves CP, NS and scale, and fixes the winning map G. Hence F>=G implies
D(F)>=G at the same scale. The unrestricted dual may therefore be restricted
to separate output eigenvalue blocks (a,b) at each setting flag (x,y).

For fixed x let u_n^(x), n>=0, be the alternating word of length n beginning
with1-x, with u_0 empty. These are representatives of the left cosets of the
subgroup generated by R_x. Put s_a=(-1)^a and

    f_(x,a),n=sqrt(2) P_(a|x) u_n^(x).

In the canonical group trace, all these features for a fixed x are
orthonormal:

    tau(f_(x,a),m^* f_(x,b),n)=delta_ab delta_mn.

This follows from orthogonality of distinct reduced words; no two chosen
coset representatives differ by left multiplication by R_x.

For each of the sixteen labels (x,a;y,b), choose a PSD Gram matrix on feature
pairs (i,j). The winning target occurs only when a=y,b=x and is

    k=(1/4)e_(0,0),   kk*=e_(0,0)e_(0,0)^*/16.

All other outcome blocks have zero winning target. The channel scale is the
sum of the sixteen Gram traces.

## 2. Exact Toeplitz/Hankel coefficient constraints

For one party the traced feature overlap is

    f_(x,a),m^* f_(x,a),n
      =u_m^*u_n+s_a u_m^* R_x u_n.                     (1)

The first reduced word has length|m-n| and the second has lengthm+n+1.
Thus the NS equations involve difference and sum indices, with exact word
orientation determined by the parity and setting label. They are not tests
on sampled angles.

At level L, use n=L+1 features per outcome. Let M have rows indexed by all
nonempty reduced words w of length at most2L+1 and columns indexed by
(x,a,m,n). Its column is the coefficient vector of (1). Then

    M M^T=diag_w[4(L+1)-2|w|].                          (2)

To prove (2), sum the two outcome signs: the cross term between the difference
and sum words cancels. For a fixed oriented word of length ell, the difference
part contributes2(n-ell) occurrences if ell<n, and the sum part contributes
min(ell,2n-ell). Their sum is2n-ell; the outcome sum supplies the final factor2.
Every row occurs, so M has rank4L+2.

Arrange the full Gram array as a matrix T indexed locally by (x,a,i,k) versus
(y,b,j,l). The exact NS constraints are

    T M^T=0,   M T=0.

Their orthogonal projection is

    T -> P T P, P=I-M^T diag_w[4n-2|w|]^-1 M.           (3)

All coefficients are rational. The uniform diagonal Gram identity across all
sixteen blocks is NS: the two output signs cancel its nonidentity overlap
terms. Thus the established rational factor/affine-projection/identity-repair
certificate method applies without an unproved positivity extension.

Game twirling leaves four error blocks H_ef, e=a xor y,f=b xor x. Reconstruction
uses the coefficient signs(-1)^(y i+x j). Party exchange identifies H_10 with
the swap of H_01, leaving H_00,H_01,H_11; the equal-error blocks split into
symmetric and antisymmetric parts. This is the half-line counterpart of the
physical three-error-block reduction.

## 3. It lies between adjacent ordinary word levels

Let b_L be the ordinary word-channel optimum and c_L this coset optimum.
A coset feature of length at most L expands into words of length at most L+1,
so b_(L+1)<=c_L. Conversely, output-dephasing a degree-L ordinary word
completion gives features P_(a|x)u. If u begins with x, remove that first
letter using P_(a|x)R_x=s_a P_(a|x); otherwise u is already a coset
representative. Hence the resulting coset level is at most L and c_L<=b_L:

    b_(L+1) <= c_L <= b_L.                              (4)

This both preserves hierarchy completeness and prevents mistaking a finite
coset truncation for a new unrestricted attainment theorem.

## 4. A single Hermitian Jacobi variable

Set

    T=R0+R1, D=R0-R1.

They satisfy T*=T, D*=D, TD=-DT, T^2+D^2=4I. In particular spec(T) is contained
in[-2,2]. Define monic polynomials

    p_0^(s)(t)=1,
    p_1^(s)(t)=t-s,
    p_(n+1)^(s)(t)=t p_n^(s)(t)-p_(n-1)^(s)(t).        (5)

Then, for BOTH settings,

    P_(a|x)u_n^(x)=P_(a|x)p_n^(s_a)(T).                (6)

For n=0, right multiplication by T produces s_a times the empty representative
plus the length-one representative. For n>=1 it produces the two neighboring
representatives. This proves (5)-(6) directly. Equivalently
p_n^(s)(t)=U_n(t/2)-s U_(n-1)(t/2), where U_n is the second-kind Chebyshev
polynomial and U_-1=0.

The canonical spectral measure for the normalized output-sign feature is

    dmu_s(t)=(1+s t/2) dt/[pi sqrt(4-t^2)], -2<t<2.

Its orthogonal-polynomial Jacobi matrix has off-diagonal entries1, boundary
diagonal entry s, and all remaining diagonal entries0. The endpoint singularity
is integrable and there is no omitted scalar representation: the formal
identities are valid at T=+/-2 as well.

## 5. NS as two ordinary univariate polynomial identities

Since R_x=(T+(-1)^x D)/2 and D p(T)=p(-T)D, equation (1), with source index n
and adjoint index m, becomes

    (1+s_a T/2) p_m^(s_a)(T)p_n^(s_a)(T)
       +(-1)^x(s_a/2) p_m^(s_a)(T)p_n^(s_a)(-T)D.      (7)

Every word polynomial has a unique normal form A(T)+B(T)D. Faithfulness follows
by evaluating T=diag(t,-t), D=sqrt(4-t^2)X for0<t<2. Thus vanishing universally
means A and B are zero polynomials, not merely zero on one finite spectrum.

For fixed untraced Alice indices (x,a,i,k), the Bob NS condition is therefore:

    sum_(y,b,j,l) H_(x,a;y,b)[i,j;k,l]
        (1+s_b t/2)p_l^(s_b)(t)p_j^(s_b)(t)
                  is constant in t,

    sum_(y,b,j,l) H_(x,a;y,b)[i,j;k,l]
        (-1)^y(s_b/2)p_l^(s_b)(t)p_j^(s_b)(-t)=0.

Exchange parties for the other NS identity. These are finite, exactly
checkable univariate polynomial equations at every finite level.

This form exposes the relevant half-line boundary condition for a rational
positive-kernel or Riccati construction. The positivity requirement is still
positivity of the FULL coherent two-angle kernel; pointwise positivity at t=s
alone is insufficient.

## 6. Finite-support annihilators

The [spectral-ideal realization proof](../finite-realization/flat_tester_realization.md#7-relation-to-the-output-dephased-jacobi-recurrence) owns the general annihilator and its explicit special-support examples. Its full ideal-annihilation and normalization hypotheses remain necessary for the stated realization theorem; coset formulas alone do not supply a positive universal extension.
